# Partner Outreach SEO Pack

Use this brief when asking partners to link to AgriMinds from their official websites, event pages, newsletters, partner directories, or press coverage.

## Recommended destination pages

- General ecosystem partnership: `https://agriminds.in/partners`
- Founding chapter and local activity: `https://agriminds.in/chapters/vizag`
- Marketplace event story: `https://agriminds.in/blog/market-place`
- AgriMinds launch story: `https://agriminds.in/blog/launch-event`
- Video coverage: `https://agriminds.in/videos`

## Suggested anchor text

Use natural, descriptive text and vary it by context:

- AgriMinds Ecosystem Foundation
- AgriMinds agriculture entrepreneurship network
- AgriMinds Vizag chapter
- AgriMinds marketplace event
- AgriMinds farmer and FPO ecosystem

Avoid repeating one exact keyword phrase on every site.

For a more specific reference, you can also link directly to our Vizag chapter or marketplace story:

https://agriminds.in/chapters/vizag
https://agriminds.in/blog/market-place

Please use the wording that best fits your own editorial style. We are happy to share a short description, event photographs, or an official logo if needed.

Regards,
AgriMinds Ecosystem Foundation

## Outreach checklist

- [ ] Confirm the partner contact and relationship owner
- [ ] Choose the most relevant destination page
- [ ] Ask for a contextual link from an existing partner, event, news, or ecosystem page
- [ ] Avoid requesting sitewide footer links
- [ ] Record the request date and response
- [ ] Verify the live link and destination after publication
